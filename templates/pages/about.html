Community Project & Platform

Empowering Local Daily Wage Workers

Unlike traditional white-collar job boards (LinkedIn, Naukri, or Indeed), RojgarSetu is engineered specifically for skilled and unskilled daily laborers, electricians, plumbers, masons, painters, housekeeping personnel, and security guards.

Eliminating Income Gaps

Daily workers often lose 30-40% of their monthly income because word-of-mouth fails between contracts. Our platform provides real-time visibility into immediate nearby requirements.

Verified Ratings & Fair Wages

Workers build long-term reputation through verified attendance and punctuality scores. Contractors gain trust and hire with confidence in under 30 minutes.

Core Values

  • Zero Commissions: 100% of the wage agreed upon goes into the worker's hands.
  • Local Proximity: Matching jobs within 2 to 10 km to minimize travel costs and exhaustion.
  • Digital Financial Inclusion: Same-day direct UPI wallet payouts and verifiable earning statements.
